# Company OS prototype

A clickable prototype of a work app where AI teammates and people work in one place. It is a design concept, not a product: nothing is connected. The sample workspace is set up as Good + Natural, a natural health store in Steinbach, Manitoba, running its marketing in the app. Some details come from the store; most numbers, tasks and conversations are sample data for the demo.

**Open it:** https://chipmartens.github.io/company-os-prototype/

## How to look around

1. **Today** is the one list of what needs you. Every count in the app reads from it.
2. **Chat** holds your private Assistant, your AI teammates and your channels.
3. The rail on the left switches workspaces: Today, Chat, Tasks, Calendar, Meetings, Partners, Reports, Library.
4. Switch between an admin (Leane) and a member (Dennis) from the avatar menu, under **Demo: view as**.

## Shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K | Search, or ask your Assistant |
| ⌘J | Ask your Assistant (right on the page you're on) |
| ⌘I | Show or hide details |
| ⌘Z | Undo the last approval while its countdown runs |
| ⌘1 to ⌘8 | Switch workspace |
| ? | Show all shortcuts (single-key ones can be turned off in the avatar menu) |
