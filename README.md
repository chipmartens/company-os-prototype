# Company OS prototype

A clickable prototype of a work app where AI teammates and people work in one place. It is a design concept, not a product: nothing is connected, and every company, person and number in it is made up (the sample company is "Halden Manufacturing").

**Open it:** https://chipmartens.github.io/company-os-prototype/

## How to look around

1. The first screen is day one: setup from a company website. Click **Skip to sample workspace** (top right) to jump to a busy sample day.
2. **Today** is the one list of what needs you. Every count in the app reads from it.
3. **Chat** holds your private Assistant, your AI teammates and your channels.
4. The rail on the left switches workspaces: Today, Chat, Tasks, Calendar, Meetings, Customers, Reports, Library.
5. Switch between an admin (Priya) and a member (Marcus) from the avatar menu, under **Demo: view as**.

## Shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| ⌘K | Search, or ask your Assistant |
| ⌘J | Ask your Assistant (right on the page you're on) |
| ⌘I | Show or hide details |
| ⌘1 to ⌘8 | Switch workspace |
| ? | Show all shortcuts |
